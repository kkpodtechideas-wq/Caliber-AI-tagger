# Library User Guide

Your personal digital library, accessible from any browser.

## Accessing the Library

Open: http://YOUR_VPS_IP:8083 and log in.

## Finding Books

### Browse by Category

Click Categories in the left sidebar to see all tags:

- genre: Browse by type (fiction, philosophy, business, history...)
- theme: Browse by concept (power, identity, consciousness...)
- topic: Browse by specific subject (options trading, marketing...)
- person: Find books discussing a specific person
- biography: Find biographies about a specific person
- mood: Filter by reading feel (academic, practical, conversational...)
- level: Filter by difficulty (introductory, intermediate, advanced)
- language: Filter by language
- era: Filter by time period

Click any tag to see all matching books.

### Search

Simple search: Type in the search bar (e.g., "Jung", "fiction")

Advanced search: Click "Advanced Search" to filter by multiple fields at once.

### Other Browse Options

- Authors - alphabetical by author
- Languages - filter by language
- Series - browse series
- Publishers - browse by publisher
- File Formats - filter by PDF, EPUB, etc.
- Discover - random suggestions

## Reading Books

1. Click any book to see its details
2. Options:
   - Read in browser (best for EPUBs)
   - Download (better for PDFs)

Tips:
- EPUBs read well in the browser with adjustable fonts
- PDFs are better downloaded and opened in a dedicated reader
- On mobile, download to Apple Books or a PDF reader

## Organizing

- Mark books as Read/Unread to track progress
- Archive books to hide from main view
- Create Shelves for custom organization (e.g., "Reading Now", "Work Reference")
